Weather	wind speed/direction, temperature, pressure, waves	Sea-ice + iceberg trajectory + risk	🔴 Essential

The SRS explicitly identifies satellite, sea-ice, meteorological, oceanographic, iceberg and AIS/vessel data as the relevant categories.

2. Start with a defined Antarctic region

This is very important.

Don't download "all Antarctica" immediately.

Your SRS recommends a narrow, demonstrable mission corridor for the prototype.

For example, choose something like:

Research corridor
     ↓
McMurdo / Ross Sea

or

Antarctic Peninsula
        ↓
South Shetland region

Then collect all datasets for approximately the same geographic bounding box.

This makes the ML problem dramatically easier.

Your first data specification should look like:

Region:
    lat: -75 to -60
    lon: 150E to 180E

Training period:
    2015–2024

Validation:
    2025

Target forecast:
    1–7 days

Don't treat those coordinates/dates as mandatory; they're an example. The important point is to freeze one region + one historical period before collecting data.
